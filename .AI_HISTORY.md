## 2026-10-30
### Per-vendor venvs under vendor/
- `VendorSetup` installs into `vendor/krkn/.venv` or
  `vendor/benchmark-runner/.venv` (from `KRKN_SRC` / `BR_SRC`), then
  `pytest_configure` re-execs under that interpreter (fixtures stay in-process).
- Vendor venvs are created with python3.11 (`VENDOR_PYTHON`), not the
  framework interpreter (avoids numpy build failures on 3.14).
- `run_parallel.py` picks the matching vendor python per job so BR and chaos
  can run together.

- Restored naming: `VendorSetup` / “one vendor at a time” (was briefly `ToolSetup`).
- Kept `src/PyTest/vendor/.gitkeep`; gitignore local checkouts under `vendor/`.
